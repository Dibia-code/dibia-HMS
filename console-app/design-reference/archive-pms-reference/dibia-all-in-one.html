Everything a pharmacy actually runs on

Not a cash register. A control room.

Every module a real pharmacy business needs — built around how stock actually moves, expires, and gets sold.

FEFO expiry control

Earliest-expiring batches sell first, automatically — with overrides logged to the audit trail, not silently allowed.

Fast, offline-capable POS

Barcode scanning, split payments, price overrides by permission — sales keep moving even when the internet doesn't.

Multi-branch, one head office

Every branch keeps its own stock and cashiers — while the CEO sees performance across all of them from one screen.

Staff accountability built in

Every discount, price change, void and adjustment is tied to the staff member who made it — nothing disappears quietly.

Batch-level accuracy

Multiple batches of the same drug tracked separately — cost, price, supplier and expiry never get merged or confused.

Real profit, not just sales

Cost of goods, expenses and margin by product, staff, branch and period — so "busy" and "profitable" don't get confused.

The thing every other system gets wrong

Keeps selling when the network cuts out.

Every sale is written to the device first, using IndexedDB — never lost, never blocked by a bad connection. The moment you're back online, everything reconciles automatically.

  • A status indicator is always visible — you always know if you're online or offline.
  • Sales made offline queue locally and sync the moment connectivity returns.
  • No duplicate transactions — sync is built to reconcile safely, every time.
