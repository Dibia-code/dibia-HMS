# Dibia Hospital Management System

Dibia HMS helps hospitals coordinate day-to-day care and administration in one connected workspace. Teams can register patients, route visits to departments, manage queues, record payments, and keep patient details available to the staff who need them.

## For hospital teams

The HMS supports reception, clinical workflows, cashier and billing, hospital pharmacy, inventory, finance, and hospital administration. Patient registration includes contact and demographic details, with optional fields that can be completed later. Pharmacy, dispensing, and stock management are hospital departments within the system.

Staff sign in to the hospital workspace and see the tools and dashboard for the role or roles assigned to them. Hospital Management controls access by managing staff roles and permissions, and can oversee hospital operations. Branch Managers have a branch operations view.

## Dibia Super Admin console

The separate console supports Dibia network administrators who manage hospital registrations, subscriptions, and support across the hospital network. It is distinct from the Hospital Management workspace used by hospital staff.

New hospital registrations wait for Dibia Super Admin approval before the hospital owner can sign in. The apps use essential, secure session cookies for sign-in; they do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

The console includes local preview credentials for demonstration only. Real administrator accounts must be authenticated by the backend when integration is enabled.
